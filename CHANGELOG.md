# Changelog

## 0.1.0
- First version of Ink Town. You're the town planner: the county gives you $20,000 and a meadow by a river, with a town hall already built.
- Build from the dock at the bottom: roads and footpaths, homes, shops, work, gardens and services. Drag to lay roads, paths and hedges, drag out flower beds and ponds, sweep your finger to plant trees, and tap to place buildings. Two fingers move and zoom the map.
- Roads must lead back to the town hall. Buildings without one show a "!" bubble and stay empty.
- People move into homes when there are jobs and shops, and when their street is pleasant. Trees, gardens, water and parks cheer people up; factories and workshops are noisy. As the town grows, people expect a clinic, a school, a fire station, a library and a police post nearby.
- The three bars at the top show what the town wants next: homes, shops or work.
- Money comes in at the end of each day from residents, businesses and shop sales, and upkeep goes out. Tap your money for the town ledger: set taxes low, fair or high, and take a bank loan if you need one.
- Tasks at the top left guide you through your first town, each with a reward. Growing from Hamlet to Village, Town, Borough and City pays a grant and unlocks new buildings.
- Undo button, three game speeds and pause, and a button to jump back to the town hall.
- Your town saves itself on this phone.
